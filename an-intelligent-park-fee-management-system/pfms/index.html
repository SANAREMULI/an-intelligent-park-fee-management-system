The problem we solve

From paper queues to a single tap

Manual fee collection at Sekenani Gate created bottlenecks, lost revenue, and frustrated visitors. We replaced it entirely.

  • ⏳
    Long queues

    Visitors waited hours at the gate during peak season, cutting into precious wildlife time.

  • 🧾
    Lost receipts & revenue leakage

    Paper tickets were misplaced or duplicated, creating gaps in revenue accounting.

  • 📊
    Inaccurate visitor records

    Manual headcounts produced unreliable data, hampering park management decisions.

  • 💳
    Cash-only payments

    No digital payment options excluded modern travellers and introduced cash-handling risk.

Project objectives

Built around four research objectives

Each objective was driven by a specific operational gap identified at Sekenani Gate.

01
Automate fee collection

Replace paper-based collection with a digital payment pipeline supporting M-Pesa STK, Visa/Mastercard, and cash reconciliation.

02
Real-time monitoring

Provide live dashboards showing daily visitor counts, revenue totals, and gate throughput to KWS management staff.

03
User-friendly interface

Design an intuitive booking flow accessible to all visitors regardless of technical background, with full mobile support.

04
Comprehensive reports

Generate audit trails, nationality breakdowns, and revenue summaries to support strategic KWS decisions.
